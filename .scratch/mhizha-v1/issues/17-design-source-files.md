## Answer

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-26: no source files are needed.

- **Layout:** the Document PDF prototype rebuilds the layout by eye from the reference PDFs (Quotation, Invoice, Receipt, letterhead), which stay in the reference designs folder outside this repo.
- **Branding:** none of it is used. No logo or watermark from the reference Business; the prototype uses a made-up Business, a placeholder logo and dummy data.
- **Fonts:** the references are flat images, so their fonts are unknown. The prototype picks free fonts with an open licence (SIL Open Font License) that can ship inside a GPL-3.0 app.
