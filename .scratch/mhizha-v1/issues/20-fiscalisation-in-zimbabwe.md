# Fiscalisation in Zimbabwe

Label: wayfinder:research
Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

Must a Business that is a sole trader not registered for VAT, fiscalise now? Can a free phone app that works offline do it later? For later updates: version 1 does not fiscalise and the spec does not wait for this.

Cover: the legal basis (Income Tax Act sections 80D, 80DD, 90; SI 104 of 2010; Finance Acts) and whether regulations are actually in force for non-VAT traders; start dates; penalties; the free ZIMRA API and mobile point of sale; device registration and certification; offline rules; fields, QR codes, numbering and day opening/closing; credit notes and cancellation; what a version 1 record must hold so that fiscalising can be added without rebuilding records. ZIMRA's "Fiscalisation Explained" page says all taxpayers must; TotalTally says non-VAT traders need not.

## Answer

Probably no duty on such a Business today (medium confidence), but ZIMRA's page says all taxpayers must. Later fiscalising by app is technically possible: the API is free and receipts can queue offline, but ZIMRA must approve the app and closing a fiscal day normally needs internet. Required VAT registration changes the answer.
