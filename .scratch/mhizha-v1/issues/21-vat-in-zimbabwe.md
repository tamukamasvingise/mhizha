# VAT in Zimbabwe

Label: wayfinder:research
Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

How does VAT work for a Business before and after it registers, and which Invoices carry VAT?

Cover: whether an unregistered trader may charge or show VAT; when registration becomes compulsory and how the US$25,000 is counted; start date; the transition (Quotations, Invoices, part-paid Invoices, Deposits held and work under way at registration); time of supply; taxable, zero-rated and exempt supplies for fencing, gates, welding, boreholes and plumbing, with parts and labour; VAT claimed back on purchases; tax invoice contents; credit notes versus cancellation; discounts; bad debts; rounding; currency; rate history. Use the Act and ZIMRA where readable; say what is secondary.

## Answer

An unregistered trader may not charge VAT. After registration, supplies whose time of supply is on or after the registration date carry 15.5%. Some Jobs crossing that date are unclear. Act text read is to about January 2024; 2025 and 2026 changes rest on secondary sources and ZIMRA notices.
